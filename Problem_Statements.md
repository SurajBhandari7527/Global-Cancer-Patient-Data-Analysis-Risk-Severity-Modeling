# ❓ Research Questions & Problem Statements

All problem statements and research questions addressed in the **Global Cancer Patient Analysis** project are listed in this document.

---

## 📋 List of Research Questions

### 1. Data Cleaning & Integrity
* **Question:** Was the dataset clean, or were missing values and duplicate rows present?

### 2. Patient Demographics
* **Question 2.1:** What age range was represented by the patients in this study?
* **Question 2.2:** How were patients distributed across male, female, and other gender categories?
* **Question 2.3:** Which countries were included in the dataset, and how were patients distributed among them?

### 3. Cancer Classification & Distribution
* **Question 3.1:** What were the most common cancer types recorded in the dataset?
* **Question 3.2:** How were patient diagnoses distributed across different cancer stages?
* **Question 3.3:** What proportion of patients was diagnosed at early stages (Stage 0 and Stage I) for each cancer type?

### 4. Economic Burden Analysis
* **Question 4.1:** What was the distribution of treatment costs among cancer patients?
* **Question 4.2:** Were treatment costs influenced by country, age group, or gender?
* **Question 4.3:** Were higher treatment costs caused by advanced cancer stages (Stage III/IV)?

### 5. Health & Lifestyle Risk Factors
* **Question 5.1:** What average levels were recorded for various health and environmental risk factors?
* **Question 5.2:** Which risk factor exerted the strongest influence on cancer severity scores?
* **Question 5.3:** Was the negative effect of smoking on cancer severity amplified by higher genetic risk?

### 6. Statistical Correlations & Survival Analysis
* **Question 6.1:** How were numerical features correlated with `Survival_Years` and `Target_Severity_Score`?
* **Question 6.2:** Was longer patient survival associated with higher treatment expenditure?
* **Question 6.3:** Were reduced survival years caused by advanced cancer stages (Stage III/IV)?

### 7. Machine Learning & Predictive Modeling
* **Question 7.1:** Could cancer severity scores be accurately predicted using Machine Learning?
* **Question 7.2:** Could patient survival years be predicted using Machine Learning?
